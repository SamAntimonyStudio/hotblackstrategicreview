04 · The proof gap
Your best case study answers
every question except the one that matters.
ANZ Sydney. 28,500sqm, 2,000 people, two Gold awards. Your flagship. Here is its entire proof structure.
What the page tells a buyer
Who. Where. When. How Big.
Then 400 words of design intent: mini city, venture lab, warm neutral tones, biophilic relief. Beautifully written, and entirely about the design.
What the page never says
Whether it worked
What ANZ was trying to fix. What changed after. Attendance, utilisation, retention, cost per seat. Not one client word. Not one number that isn't a dimension.
That's the metadata a designer files a project under. It isn't the evidence a procurement panel scores.

07 · Our point of view
Hot Black doesn't need more content.
It needs to stop giving away the evidence.
You already did the hard part. The work exists, it's excellent, and the toughest clients in the country have validated it. What's missing is the layer that turns thirteen years of projects into an argument the market can read without you in the room.
Not this
A posting schedule
More frequency changes the volume, not the substance. You'd be louder and just as hard to score.
But this
A proof system
Outcomes captured on every project, published where you own them, built for the panel that scores you. Channels are distribution, not strategy.
Then this
Your own instrument
The global practices do this on budgets you don't have. Nobody has built a sharp version at thirty people. That seat is open at your scale.
